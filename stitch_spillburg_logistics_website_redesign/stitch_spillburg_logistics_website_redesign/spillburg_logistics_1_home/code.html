Our Structured Five-Step Process

A disciplined lifecycle designed to eliminate shipping demurrage, verify trade compliance, and guarantee prompt consignee handovers.

01 STEP

Requirement Assessment

Evaluation of cargo volume, packing specifications, hazardous classifications (IMDG), target arrival timelines, and commercial Incoterms.

✓ CARGO DENSITY CHECK
02 STEP

Multi-Modal Planning

Optimized carrier route selection balancing sea-transit duration vs. vessel schedule integrity. Allocation of vessel slots and container equipment.

✓ ROUTE MATRIX SELECTION
03 STEP

Dispatch & Clearance

Factory collection, terminal gate entry, Sri Lanka Customs physical/document examination, draft B/L issuance, and vessel loading supervision.

✓ EDI MANIFEST FILING
04 STEP

Proactive Tracking

Live vessel AIS monitoring, transshipment port transfer verification, and advance notification of ETA adjustments directly to your operations desk.

✓ REAL-TIME AIS TRACKING
05 FINAL

Final Mile Handover

Destination port de-vanning, destination customs liaison, drayage transport, and Proof of Delivery (POD) execution with digital bill surrender.
